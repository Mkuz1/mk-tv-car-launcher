### What's new (2.9.3)

- Car: Home and Work start Google Maps' own Home / Work shortcuts (your saved places); the "No internet" check is made by the app, not the page; the Cast button left the sidebar (it's in the Control Center); split screen gives slow head units more time; the hidden update card no longer peeks at the bottom of the screen.

### 2.9.2 · helper 1.3.2

- Drive screen: satellite map; the "No internet" note shows only when Google really can't be reached; Home and Work open directions to the places saved in your Google Maps; Cast left the media panel.
- Now Playing: next / previous work with YouTube (sent as media keys when the app has no skip), and the title follows a new clip within a few seconds.

### 2.9.1

- **Car:** installs again on head units that refused 2.8.3–2.9.0 ("There was a problem parsing the package"): the car app no longer asks for the install permission and carries only what 2.8.2 had. Its Update saves the checked APK in Download and opens your file manager to install it.

### 2.9.0 · phone 1.9.0

- **In-app updates** for the TV, car and phone apps (and MK Car Helper): a card offers each new version; Update downloads, checks and installs it with Android's confirmation. Car: Control Center → Updates; phone: Customize → Updates.
- The Google Play bundles are now in one zip, so a device can't pick an `.aab` by mistake.

### Earlier in the car launcher (2.6 – 2.8.3)

- **2.8.3 / phone 1.8.0:** much faster start: app icons are kept as files instead of being drawn again every time, and the phone launcher shows its wallpaper the moment Home is pressed.

- **2.8.2 / helper 1.3.1:** the Drive screen's map no longer says "No internet" when the car is online, and Now Playing follows the app actually playing (YouTube Music, Spotify…) instead of a phone-link app's empty player.

- **Drive screen** (swipe left from Home, like CarPlay): a big Google map with your speed and one-tap Home, Work, Mosque and Petrol, beside **Now Playing** for any app (YouTube, Spotify, Anghami…).
- **Control Center** in Liquid Glass (sidebar button or swipe down): notifications, Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, brightness and volume sliders, day/night, Cast, File Transfer. With MK Car Helper 1.3 it replaces Android's old pull-down shade on the home screen.
- **File Transfer**: scan a QR code with your phone to browse the car's storage, upload into any folder, download, rename and delete over Wi‑Fi (your phone's hotspot, or the car's own Wi‑Fi).
- **Cast**: opens the AirPlay / Google Cast receiver app on the head unit (AirScreen, ZLink, Autokit…).
- **Split screen**: a split left behind after pressing Home is now closed reliably (MK Car Helper 1.1+).
- Home page: the azkar card takes the whole left side; Qibla is a gold compass button by the clock.
