Questions before you install

Is MK Launcher free?

Yes. Every app is free, has no ads and needs no account. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT license.

Which head units does the car launcher work on?

Android car head units running Android 8 or newer. It is laid out for wide 12 inch screens such as 1920 × 720 and also fits other sizes, with touch first controls.

Android says "There was a problem parsing the package"

Install the .apk file for your device, never the Google Play bundles zip. If the error stays, allow "Install unknown apps" for the file manager you open it with, then try again.

Why is it not on Google Play?

For now it is installed from the files on this page. Once installed, each app checks for new versions by itself and offers them inside the app, so you only download from here once.

What does MK Car Helper do?

It is a small optional app for the car. It opens split screen pairs, shows Now Playing for any music or video app, brings your notifications into the Control Center and swaps Android's old pull down shade for the Liquid Glass one. Switch on MK Split Screen in Accessibility after installing.

How are prayer times calculated?

From your location with the official calculation method of the country you are in. In the car the times follow the car's GPS as you travel.

Will I lose my layout when I update?

No. Updates keep your pages, folders and widgets. The phone launcher can also save a backup to Downloads and restore it on a new device.

Can I go back to my old launcher?

Any time. Choose another Home app in Android's settings under Default apps, or uninstall MK Launcher like any other app.
