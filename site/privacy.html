Privacy

MK Launcher has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in the apps is sent to MK.

Location

Your location is used on the device to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not uploaded anywhere by the launcher. On the TV, your rough location is sent to Open‑Meteo to get the weather.

Updates

Once a day each app asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, and downloads it from GitHub when you tap Update.

Google Maps

The car's Drive screen shows a Google map. If you add your own Google Maps key, the map and directions come from Google under your key and Google's own privacy policy.

File Transfer and notifications

File Transfer runs only on your local network, protected by a key in the QR code, and only after you start it. MK Car Helper reads what is playing and your notifications to show them in the Control Center; they stay on the head unit.

This website

This site sets no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and the latest version number from GitHub.
